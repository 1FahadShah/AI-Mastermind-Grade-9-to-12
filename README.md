# AI Mastermind Program

> **Track B (Grades 9-12)** | **25 Sessions** | **8 Modules** | **25 Hours** | **1:1 Online AI Mentoring**

---

## Overview

AI Mastermind is a 25-session AI learning program designed for students in Grades 9-12.

The program introduces learners to the foundations of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Generative AI, Prompting, AI Domains, Real-World AI, Responsible AI, and a final research-based Capstone Project.

The program combines conceptual learning with hands-on exploration, research, experimentation, and practical application. Learners progress from understanding foundational AI concepts to examining modern AI systems, exploring AI applications and limitations, and developing an independent research project.

---

## Program Goals

The program is designed to help learners:

- Understand foundational AI concepts
- Explore how machines learn from data
- Understand Generative AI and Large Language Models
- Understand how modern language models process and generate text
- Develop practical prompting skills
- Explore language, vision, voice, and multimodal AI
- Understand how AI is used in everyday life and across industries
- Explore AI's relationship with careers and future work
- Recognize issues involving bias, fairness, safety, misuse, privacy, and governance
- Conduct an AI-related research project
- Research and evaluate relevant sources
- Conduct hands-on AI exploration
- Document observations and evidence
- Analyze findings through responsible AI considerations
- Communicate research findings through a research paper and presentation
- Document AI use and attribution
- Defend and reflect on their research work

## Module Breakdown

### Module 01 - Foundations of Artificial Intelligence

**Sessions 01-02**

Introduces the fundamental ideas behind Artificial Intelligence and the basic concepts used to describe AI systems.

Learners explore:

- What AI is
- AI in everyday life
- Sense → Think → Act
- AI vs human intelligence
- Common AI myths
- Narrow AI vs General AI
- Data
- Algorithms
- Training
- Models
- Predictions

The module establishes the terminology and concepts used throughout the rest of the program.

---

### Module 02 - How Machines Learn?

**Sessions 03-06**

Introduces Machine Learning and how AI systems learn patterns from data.

Learners explore:

- Machine Learning
- Supervised Learning
- Unsupervised Learning
- Reinforcement Learning
- Datasets
- Features
- Training and testing
- Generalization
- Overfitting
- Bias in data
- Training AI models

Learners also work with datasets and train an AI model through hands-on exploration.

---

### Module 03 - Generative AI & Modern Models

**Sessions 07-10**

Introduces Generative AI and modern AI models.

Learners explore:

- Generative AI
- Large Language Models
- Next-word prediction
- Tokens
- Attention
- Parameters
- Context windows
- AI capabilities
- AI limitations
- Hallucinations
- Bias
- Fact-checking AI outputs

The module also examines why language models can produce human-like outputs while still having important limitations.

---

### Module 04 - Prompting & Human-AI Interaction

**Sessions 11-13**

Develops practical prompting skills and applies them to different tasks.

Learners explore:

- Prompting fundamentals
- Prompt structure
- Prompting techniques
- Advanced prompting
- Tool selection
- Prompt testing
- Prompt improvement
- Creative tasks
- Everyday tasks
- Research tasks
- Learning tasks
- Writing tasks
- Responsible AI use

The module progresses from understanding prompting techniques to testing and applying them to practical situations.

---

### Module 05 - AI Domains & Modality

**Sessions 14-17**

Introduces different AI domains and modalities through which AI can process and generate information.

Learners explore:

- Natural Language Processing
- Computer Vision
- Voice AI
- Multimodal AI
- Text
- Images
- Audio
- Video
- Code

Hands-on activities allow learners to explore different AI modalities and examine how AI systems work with different types of information.

---

### Module 06 - AI in Real World

**Sessions 18-19**

Explores how AI is used in everyday life, across industries, and in the world of work.

Learners explore:

- AI applications
- Healthcare
- Finance
- Transportation
- Entertainment
- Education
- Human-in-the-loop
- AI augmentation
- AI and careers
- Future of work
- AI literacy
- Critical thinking
- Creativity
- Communication
- Problem solving

Learners also explore AI applications through practical interaction with a no-code AI application builder.

---

### Module 07 - Responsible AI

**Sessions 20-21**

Introduces responsible AI through bias, fairness, safety, misuse, privacy, and governance.

Learners explore:

- AI bias
- Fairness
- Data bias
- Algorithmic bias
- Human bias
- AI auditing
- AI safety
- AI misuse
- Deepfakes
- Privacy
- Safeguards
- Human oversight
- Rules and policies
- AI governance
- Accountability

Learners examine AI-related cases and scenarios to identify risks, consider safeguards, and understand the role of human oversight.

---

### Module 08 - Capstone Project

**Sessions 22-25**

Learners progress through:

The capstone includes:

- Project scope
- Source gathering
- Deep literature reading
- Research evidence
- Hands-on AI exploration
- Methodology
- Results
- User testing

- Synthesis

- Safety & Governance analysis

- References

- Final reflection

## Learning Experience

Each instructional session is supported by a consistent set of learning materials.

### Teaching Deck

The instructor-led presentation used during the session.

### Study Material

The learner-facing reference containing the concepts, examples, explanations, and activities covered in the session.

### Assessment Quiz

A session-level knowledge check used across Sessions 01-21.

### Capstone Work

Sessions 22-25 are research-paper-based and do not use separate assessment quizzes.

Progress is demonstrated through the ongoing development of the research project, research evidence, practical investigation, presentation, defense, and final reflection.

## Capstone & Research

The final four sessions form one continuous research project rather than four independent lessons.

Learners begin by defining a research direction and research question.

They then conduct deeper literature reading and hands-on AI exploration, document their findings, develop methodology and results, and conduct user testing where applicable.

The project is then examined through responsible AI considerations before the learner completes the research paper, presentation, defense, and reflection.

The capstone also requires learners to document their use of AI and distinguish their own contributions from AI-assisted work.

---

## Responsible AI Throughout the Program

Responsible AI is introduced explicitly in Module 07 and continues into the Capstone Project.

Learners encounter questions around:

- Bias
- Fairness
- Safety
- Misuse
- Privacy
- Governance
- Ethics
- Human involvement
- AI attribution
- Research integrity

The capstone applies these considerations through five analysis lenses:

- Bias & Fairness
- Safety & Governance
- Ethics
- Human-in-the-Loop
- Voice Integrity
